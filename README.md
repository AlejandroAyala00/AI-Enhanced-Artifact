## 💡 What is GreenLink?
GreenLink is a sustainability analytics web app that helps **businesses, governments, and nonprofits** track their carbon and water savings.  
It delivers **actionable insights** to improve efficiency, transparency, and environmental performance.

---
## 🧭 Why It Delivers Business Value
- **Efficiency:** Helps identify energy/water inefficiencies.
- **Accountability:** Provides measurable sustainability metrics.
- **Decision Support:** Offers data visualization for leadership teams.
- **Reputation:** Supports ESG reporting and sustainability goals.

---
## 🧰 How to Use
1. Visit the `index.html` homepage.
2. Click on **Dashboard** to view live sustainability metrics.
3. Contact section enables partnership inquiries or demo requests.
