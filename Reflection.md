# 🤖 Reflection on Using AI for GreenLink Development

## 🧠 Brainstorming Summary
AI and I brainstormed several project ideas including a civic service tracker and donation portal.  
We ultimately chose **GreenLink** because it had **clear business value**, broad applicability, and measurable impact.

**Snippet Example:**
> "Make it a sustainability tracker for organizations — something both nonprofit and corporate leaders could use."

## 💭 Why I Chose This Artifact
I wanted an artifact that demonstrates how data visualization can support **sustainability strategy and efficiency tracking**, which is increasingly relevant across industries.

## ⚙️ What Worked
- AI accelerated content generation for HTML/JS structure.
- Tailwind + Chart.js combination simplified responsive UI creation.
- GitHub workflow automation reduced manual steps.

## 🧩 What Didn’t Work
- Some AI layout suggestions required human correction for responsiveness.
- Color contrast and ARIA labeling were fine-tuned manually.

## 📘 What I Learned
Using AI effectively requires **human guidance** to ensure quality and alignment with real-world business goals.  
I learned that AI is best at scaffolding and speeding up implementation — **but business context still matters most**.
